# Code of conduct

We want AI Hub to be a friendly, useful place for everyone, whatever their experience, background or the AI tool they prefer.

## Expected

- Be kind and patient. Assume good intent; explain, do not belittle.
- Give and accept constructive feedback; focus on the work, not the person.
- Respect different tools and workflows: Claude Code, Codex, Antigravity CLI, local models and others are all welcome here.
- Keep private data out of issues and pull requests (keys, tokens, personal notes, other people's data).

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Spam, or posting skills/agents meant to deceive or harm users.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove contributions and may block people who break these rules. To report a problem, use GitHub's private reporting (Security tab) or contact the maintainers through the repository.

This text is adapted in spirit from the Contributor Covenant (<https://www.contributor-covenant.org>).
